# English Hub ✎🎯⚡🧮

Dictation, vocabulary quiz, tense-drill and noun-sorting games, built from my own
English books (Master Vocab · 12-English-Tenses Situation Guide).

- **QuizWiz 🎯** — vocabulary quiz games from Master Vocab (A1–C1 + EXTRA):
  pick your level, filter words by first letters A–Z (multi-select or ALL),
  choose a game and play. Seven games — **word → meaning**, **meaning → word**,
  **listen & guess** (hear the word, pick it), **missing word** (example with a
  gap), **scramble** (unscramble the letters), **true or false**, and a
  **60-second speed round**. Every answer shows a reveal card (word, IPA,
  meanings, example, synonyms, 🔊) — wrong words become *tricky* and come back
  in mixed review until you master them. Voice/speed shared with WriteRight;
  streak and stats sync into the Hub.
- **WriteRight ✎** — listen & write dictation: words, sentences, meanings (hear the
  meaning → write the word), pronunciation clinic (stress + common-mistake notes),
  or paste your own article. Type it (auto-check with letter diff) or write it in
  your book and reveal. Spaced review brings missed words back (1 → 3 → 7 → 14
  days), daily streak, voice picker, Apple Pencil support, A–Z letter filter,
  one-tap backup/restore.
- **Pattern Reflex ⚡** — situation / question → answer-pattern reflex. A
  situation appears and you choose the pattern (formula + this-situation
  example, no tense names until you answer). Four modes — **In this
  situation…** (700 everyday moments), **Answer this question** (200 Q → A
  cards), **Make the question** (100) and **Active → Passive** (200: you read
  an ACTIVE sentence and pick the passive pattern that keeps its tense — “be”
  carries the tense, the main verb is V₃). Every situation card first explains
  the moment (who is speaking · when · what you want to say), shows the line to
  build with a gap, and hides a one-line clue behind a 💡 button; the feedback
  then explains the moment (38 situation types: habit, scene-setting, fresh
  news, deadline…), the clue, and why the pattern you picked doesn't fit this
  moment. Positives AND negatives, random options every play, wrong answers
  come back until you nail them. Data is generated by
  `build_situation_reflex.py` from `reflex_types.json` + `reflex_ctx/`.
- **Count & Measure 🧮** — countable & uncountable nouns. Ten games - **Sort it**
  (countable or uncountable? 231 nouns, incl. "both" nouns such as *a coffee /
  coffee* in a sentence), **Make it plural** (apple → apples, baby → babies,
  knife → knives — and uncountables have none), **A, an or some**, **Some or
  any?**, **Much or many?**, **A few or a little?**, **A … of** (measure words: a
  piece of advice, a slice of bread), **Is or are?**, **Right or wrong?** (judge
  a whole sentence) and **Find the mistake** (tap the word that is wrong) - plus
  a **60-second speed round**. Every answer shows the why, the picture (things
  you count one by one vs an amount you measure), examples you can hear and the
  classic mistake; wrong answers can be redone, tricky cards come back. 1,020
  cards, levels A1-C2. Data is generated by `build_count_game.py`. Stats sync
  into the Hub.

No server, no build step — plain HTML/CSS/JS. Progress and themes are saved in
your browser. Installable (PWA) and works offline.

Deployed on GitHub Pages from `main`.
